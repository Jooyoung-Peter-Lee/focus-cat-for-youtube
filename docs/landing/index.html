1. Overview

Focus Cat for YouTube ("Focus Cat", "the extension", "we", "us") is a Chrome browser extension developed and operated as an independent project. This Privacy Policy explains what information is involved when you use Focus Cat and what your rights are.

2. Information stored locally on your device

The following data is stored using Chrome's chrome.storage.local API. It lives only on your device and is not accessible to us:

  • Your daily YouTube watch time (stored as milliseconds)
  • Your settings: daily limit, focus mode, content filtering preferences
  • Your Pro license status: active or inactive

3. Information transmitted to our servers

When you activate or deactivate a Pro license key, the license key string is sent to our license verification endpoint (hosted on Vercel) to confirm its validity with Lemon Squeezy. No other data is transmitted. We do not log your IP address, device information, browsing history, or YouTube usage.

We do not collect:

  • YouTube watch history or video titles
  • Browsing history of any kind
  • Personal identity information
  • Analytics, telemetry, or usage data

Focus Cat Pro is sold through Lemon Squeezy, a Merchant of Record. When you purchase, Lemon Squeezy collects and processes your payment details and email address under their own privacy policy. We receive a license key and a purchase confirmation for fulfillment purposes only.

6. Data retention

All locally stored data is retained until you uninstall Focus Cat or manually clear the extension's storage via Chrome settings. License activation status is retained until you deactivate the key.

7. Children's privacy

Focus Cat is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.
